# Multi_community-local-services-hub
# "PROJECT TITLE: Agnes Local service Hub."
The hub helps to improves access to mutil local services by providing a centralized and easy-to-use platform. 

#" Problem Statement"
Many residents and visitors in Kampala struggle to find reliable, up-to-date information about essential local services across different sectors. Existing platforms are often cluttered, contain outdated contact details, or focus only on high-end tourism. There is a lack of a centralized, user-friendly portal that categorizes everyday essentials—like pharmacies, banks, and hardware stores—while allowing users to move directly from discovery to action (booking,or get direction) without navigating away from the page.

#"Targeted Users"
The Agnes Local Service Hub is designed for:
•	Local Residents: People looking for the nearest reliable pharmacy, bank, or supermarket in their specific neighborhood (e.g., Ntinda, Bugolobi).
•	Students: High school graduates and continuing students seeking information on universities and educational institutions.
•	Business Professionals: Individuals looking for corporate services, banks, or meeting venues (hotels).
•	Newcomers & Expats: People new to Kampala who need a trusted directory to navigate essential services like hospitals and supermarkets.
•	Small Business Owners: Local vendors who need to be discovered by a wider digital audience.

#"Main Objectives"
•	Centralization: To provide a single "one-stop-shop" for 9 critical service categories in Kampala.
•	Streamlined User Experience: To eliminate "search fatigue" by using a clean, category-pill interface that shows exactly 6 top options per category.
•	Action-Oriented Design: To shift from a "read-only" directory to an interactive platform by replacing static print options with an active "Book Now" feature.
•	Information Accuracy: To ensure every listed service has a verified location, description, and rating to build user trust.
•	Visual Accessibility: To use a high-quality visual layout (images for every service) that helps users recognize establishments before they visit.

#"Setup Instructions"

**Images:** Ensure all images are placed inside the `/images` folder. If an image is missing, the system will automatically show a placeholder.
**File Naming:** Do not rename `style-3.css` or `script-3.js` unless you update the `<link>` and `<script>` tags in `index-2.html`.
**Launch:** Simply open `index-2.html` in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, or Safari).

## Customization ##

**To add more services:** Open `script-3.js` and add a new object `{ name: "...", location: "...", ... }` to the desired category array.
**To change colors:** Update the `:root` variables in `style-3.css` (e.g., `--green`).
